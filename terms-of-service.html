Section 02

Scope & Nature of Advisory Services

The Edu Consultant functions as an independent, professional educational consultancy and academic counseling agency. Our services encompass:

  • Institutional Quota Advisory: Guidance on legitimate, officially sanctioned Management Quota, NRI Quota, and institutional direct admissions in recognized private universities and autonomous colleges.
  • Fee Structure Verification: Dissemination of verified tuition costs, college infrastructure fees, hostel charges, and official refund policies approved by respective institutional management bodies.
  • Application & Dossier Processing: Assistance in compiling application files, eligibility certificates, equivalence clearances, and NRI sponsorship affidavits.
  • Global University Placement: Counseling for overseas degree programs across USA, UK, Canada, Australia, and Germany, including visa documentation advice.
Section 03

Institutional Autonomy & Regulatory Transparency

Important Admissions Disclaimer: All final seat allotments, eligibility confirmations, and admission order releases remain the sole prerogative of the respective autonomous university or college admissions authority, subject to statutory norms (e.g. AICTE, UGC, NMC, BCI, COA).

The Edu Consultant acts strictly within the bounds of authorized counseling. We do not conduct examinations, we do not alter state rank lists, and we operate strictly within the legal framework of private institutional management quotas sanctioned by the Supreme Court of India precedents.

Section 04

Applicant Responsibilities & Document Authenticity

Candidates and their guardians guarantee that:

  • All academic mark sheets, entrance scorecard numbers, transfer certificates, and category claims submitted for evaluation are genuine and issued by accredited educational boards.
  • Any misrepresentation, counterfeit certificates, or fraudulent documents will result in immediate termination of counseling advisory without right of refund.
  • The candidate meets the mandatory eligibility percentage specified by the target institution (e.g., minimum 45-50% in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology for professional degrees).
Section 05

Tuition & University Fee Guidelines

We uphold strict financial ethics and transparency:

  • Direct College Payment: All formal course tuition fees, college development fees, and hostel deposits are payable directly to the designated official bank account of the target college or university (via Demand Draft, RTGS/NEFT, or official institution ERP payment portal).
  • Zero Cash Dispatches: The Edu Consultant never accepts unaccounted cash for college tuition fees. Candidates receive official printed institutional receipts for every rupee remitted to the academic institution.
  • Consultancy Advisory Charges: Any nominal advisory or document processing fee charged directly by The Edu Consultant is clearly invoiced and acknowledged via formal digital documentation.
Section 06

Cancellation, Withdrawal & Refund Policy

College tuition fee refunds upon withdrawal are governed entirely by the individual university's statutory refund framework, in strict compliance with UGC and AICTE refund regulations (deductions based on notification date prior to the formal closure of admissions).

Professional advisory service retainers remitted directly to The Edu Consultant cover personalized research, dossier preparation, and counselor time, and become non-refundable once an official institutional seat offer or provisional allotment letter has been secured.
