Everything you need to know about direct management quota admissions, eligibility criteria, fee transparency, and free diagnostic counseling.

Management Quota is an officially sanctioned institutional intake (typically 15% to 25% of seats) approved by the State Government and university trusts for direct merit-cum-institutional admissions. It empowers eligible students who did not secure top cut-off ranks in competitive exams like KCET or COMEDK to secure verified seats across top autonomous colleges, premier private universities, and accredited engineering, medical, and management institutions in Bangalore based on their 12th/qualifying examination marks.

Institutional management and NRI quota seats are allotted primarily based on qualifying 12th Board examination marks (typically requiring a minimum aggregate of 50-60% in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology depending on the college and stream), rather than high entrance examination ranks.

All seat allotments are 100% genuine and processed directly through the respective institution's registrar and admissions office. Students and parents receive formal allotment letters, fee receipts bearing official college seals, and registered university enrollment numbers.

Key documents include: 10th and 12th Standard Marks Cards, Transfer Certificate (TC), Migration Certificate (for non-Karnataka/ICSE/CBSE students), Valid Government Photo ID (Aadhaar / Passport), Recent Passport-size Photographs, and Entrance Admit/Score Card (if appeared).
